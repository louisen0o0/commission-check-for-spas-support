Privacy Policy — Commission Check for Spas

Effective date: September 22, 2026

Commission Check for Spas is designed as a local-first business utility.

Data the app may process

The app may process files you choose to import, including membership and service/class sales reports. These files can contain business records such as customer names, staff names, service names, membership plan information, transaction identifiers, dates, commission amounts, and related commission-checking fields.

The app uses the fields needed to perform commission verification.

Where data is stored

Imported working data and check history are stored locally on your device.

The app does not require an account or a backend service.

Data transmission

The app does not send imported business data to our servers because it has no backend service.

Exports

When you choose to export a CSV or PDF, the app creates a temporary local export file so that you can use the iOS/iPadOS Share Sheet.

The destination you select is controlled by you and may be another app, cloud storage provider, AirDrop target, Files location, or another system destination.

The app cleans up temporary export files after the share flow or when the app becomes active again, subject to normal operating-system behavior.

Deletion

The app provides controls to clear check history and to clear all local app data.

Third-party software

The app uses third-party open-source libraries for supported file parsing and archive handling. Required third-party notices are included in the app bundle.

Children

The app is a business utility and is not directed to children.

Changes

If the app later adds accounts, cloud sync, analytics, collaboration, payments, or other network services, this policy will be updated before those features are released.
